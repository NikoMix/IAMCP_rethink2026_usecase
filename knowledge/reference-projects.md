# Reference projects – Fabrikam Consulting GmbH (fictional)

> the IAMCP GitHub hands-on workshop. Any resemblance to real organisations is coincidental.
> The page deliberately names no people.

**Rules for the proposal agent**

- Use only the references on this page. Never invent a reference, customer name or figure.
- Respect the "Reference use" field. If it says "anonymised only", describe the customer by

## REF-2025-01 AI knowledge assistant for field service

- **Customer:** Adventure Works Logistics AG (fictional), logistics, about 4,000 employees
- **Reference use:** may be named
- **Period:** 2025-03 to 2025-05 (8 weeks)
- **Services:** SVC-01 Discovery Workshop, SVC-06 Generative AI Proof of Concept
- **Team:** 4 people (AI Engineer, Solution Architect, Data Scientist, Engagement Manager)
- **Challenge:** Service technicians spent a large share of each call searching manuals in several languages.
- **Solution:** Agent on Azure AI Foundry grounded on 1,200 service manuals with file search,
  answer citations and an evaluation pipeline.
- **Outcome (fictional figures):** 38 % shorter average search time in the pilot group;
  the recommendation for production was accepted.

## REF-2025-02 Cloud landing zone for a regional healthcare provider

- **Customer:** Northwind Health Services (fictional), healthcare, about 2,500 employees
- **Reference use:** anonymised only
- **Period:** 2025-01 to 2025-03 (10 weeks)
- **Services:** SVC-02 Cloud Readiness Assessment, SVC-03 Landing Zone Setup, SVC-08 Security and Compliance Review
- **Team:** 5 people (Cloud Architect, two DevOps Engineers, Security Consultant, Project Manager)
- **Challenge:** Strict data protection requirements blocked the move of new workloads to the cloud.
- **Solution:** Policy-governed landing zone as infrastructure as code with private networking,
  central logging and documented compliance evidence.
- **Outcome (fictional figures):** first three workloads migrated within six weeks of handover;
  audit preparation effort reduced by an estimated 30 %.

## REF-2024-03 Data platform for retail analytics

- **Customer:** Tailspin Retail GmbH (fictional), retail, about 900 employees
- **Reference use:** may be named
- **Period:** 2024-06 to 2024-11 (5 months)
- **Services:** SVC-05 Data Platform, SVC-09 Training and Enablement
- **Team:** 6 people (Solution Architect, three Data Engineers, Business Analyst, Project Manager)
- **Challenge:** Sales and stock data lived in separate systems; weekly reports were assembled by hand.

  40 business users trained.

## REF-2024-04 Modernisation of a customer portal

- **Customer:** Wide World Insurance (fictional), insurance, about 6,000 employees
- **Reference use:** anonymised only
- **Period:** 2024-02 to 2024-08 (6 months)
- **Services:** SVC-04 Application Modernisation, SVC-07 DevOps and CI/CD Enablement, SVC-10 Hypercare
- **Team:** 7 people (Solution Architect, two Senior Developers, two Developers, DevOps Engineer, Test Engineer)
- **Challenge:** A monolithic portal could only be released quarterly and failed under peak load.
- **Solution:** Re-platforming to containerised services with automated tests, blue-green

- **Outcome (fictional figures):** release frequency increased from quarterly to weekly;
  no unplanned outage during the following renewal season.
